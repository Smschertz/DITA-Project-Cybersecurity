**Analysis**  
We’ve organized our cybersecurity documentation into a hierarchical DITA map structure with the goal of helping NC State staff, faculty, and students find information based on their specific needs. We separated the content into the twelve cybersecurity topics that we originally selected for the project and arranged the tasks, concepts, and references in an effort to support the way users might navigate through the documentation. We wanted to make sure that the organization of the structure map would support the NC State community in their efforts to understand cybersecurity concepts, navigate steps in a guided task, and refer to supporting information, without seeing too much unrelated content.

We tried to strategically connect topics that supported related documentation or provided useful context. For example, we connected the separate topics of Gmail alert messages and the process of reporting phishing, since the topic of Gmail alert messages can provide useful context for the process of reporting phishing. We also connected the topic of network access and VPN instructions since both topics similarly provide helpful context to one another. 

Our goal with the reltable was also to ensure we were connecting topics in a way that controlled how users encountered related topics and information. We used the sourceonly and targetonly attributes to make sure the relationships between topics were directional, so some content is only suggested for certain source topics. We did include one relationship that went both ways, but most of the relationships we identified only flowed in a single direction. 

We used suggestions from Gemini specifically to identify which documentation might support other topics, and to consider potential relationships that could be built into the reltable. However, we were cautious to take its suggestions at face-value and made sure to review the identified relationships to ensure they were actually relevant to both the documentation and the intended audiences. For the most part, the suggestions were helpful, although we identified additional relationships that could be made, and we did not include all of the relationships that Gemini suggested.
